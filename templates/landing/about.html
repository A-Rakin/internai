Our Mission

Bridging the Gap Between Academia and Industry

InternAI was built to solve the challenges students, companies, and academic supervisors face during the internship process. Our AI-powered platform streamlines every step — from finding the right opportunity to submitting final evaluations.

We believe that internships are the foundation of professional growth, and managing them shouldn't be a burden. InternAI automates the tedious parts so everyone can focus on what matters — learning, mentoring, and building careers.
