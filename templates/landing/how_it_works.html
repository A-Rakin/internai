How InternAI Works

A simple 4-step process for each user role to get started

For Students

Your Internship Journey

1
Register & Profile

Create your account, fill in academic details, upload your resume, and add skills to your profile.

2
Browse & Apply

Search internship listings, filter by skills and location, and submit applications with cover letters.

3
Interview & Accept

Attend scheduled interviews, receive offers from companies, and accept your preferred internship.

4
Report & Evaluate

Submit weekly reports, receive supervisor feedback, and complete your internship successfully.

For Companies

Recruitment Made Easy

1
Register Company

Create your company account, add organization details, and verify your profile.

2
Post Internships

Create detailed internship listings with requirements, skills needed, and compensation details.

3
Review & Interview

Use AI to rank candidates, review applications, schedule interviews, and select the best fit.

4
Manage Interns

Track intern performance, communicate with supervisors, and provide final evaluations.
