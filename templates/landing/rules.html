Our Commitment to Integrity & Safety

InternAI connects students, hiring companies, and academic supervisors. To safeguard our ecosystem against fraud, spam, and financial exploits, all accounts and subscription packages are subject to strict compliance oversight. Violating these rules will result in tier revocation or permanent account suspension.

01

1. Identity & Verification Integrity

Users must provide authentic contact details, university credentials, and commercial company information. Impersonating official institutions, using fake company profiles, or registering with disposable emails is strictly prohibited.

Penalty: Immediate Permanent Account Suspension & Profile Removal
02

2. Subscription & Payment Integrity

Submitting invalid or fabricated bKash, Nagad, or Upay Transaction IDs during checkout, or attempting cross-role package exploits (such as Companies attempting to subscribe to Student plans) is a violation of our financial terms.

Penalty: Instant Package Revocation & Reversion to Basic Free Tier
03

3. Internship Posting Ethics

Companies must post legitimate, verifiable internship opportunities. Demanding application fees, training payments, or money from student candidates, or publishing deceptive stipend information is grounds for immediate legal and administrative action.

Penalty: Permanent Company Ban & Placement Blacklisting
04

4. Academic Integrity & Verification

Students must accurately report internship hours and activities. Falsifying academic supervisor email addresses, forging weekly report submissions, or manipulating performance evaluation scores is strictly forbidden.

Penalty: Voiding of Career Packages & University Notification
05

5. Professional Conduct & Anti-Spam

Unsolicited bulk messaging, promotional spam, harassment, or abusive communication directed towards candidates, employers, or academic supervisors will not be tolerated.

Penalty: Direct Messaging Suspension & Account Audit
06

6. Security & Platform Safeguards

Automated scraping, bot exploitation, vulnerability probing, or sharing account access credentials with unauthorized commercial third parties is strictly prohibited.

Penalty: IP Blacklisting & Immediate Security Termination

Have Questions or Need an Appeal?

If your package was revoked or account restricted, you may submit a formal appeal to our compliance administration team.
