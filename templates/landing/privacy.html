Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, academic details, professional information, and uploaded documents such as resumes and certificates.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve our internship management services, match students with relevant opportunities, facilitate communication between users, and send important notifications.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information may be shared with companies you apply to and supervisors assigned to your internship, as necessary for the internship management process.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings. You can also contact us to exercise your data protection rights.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@internai.com.
