Strict Platform Rules & Policy Guidelines

To maintain a trustworthy ecosystem for students, companies, and academic supervisors, InternAI enforces strict violation policies. Accounts or packages are suspended or revoked upon violating any of the following rules:

1. Identity & Verification Integrity

Submitting fake company profiles, fraudulent university details, or impersonating an official organization results in immediate account suspension and tier revocation.

2. Subscription & Payment Integrity

Submitting fake bKash/Nagad/Upay Transaction IDs, or attempting cross-role package exploits (e.g. Companies purchasing Student plans) results in instant package forfeiture.

3. Internship Posting Ethics

Publishing scam internship listings, requiring money or application fees from student candidates, or misleading stipend terms results in permanent company ban.

4. Academic Integrity & Verification

Falsifying university supervisor email addresses, forging weekly report hours, or manipulating evaluation records will void student accounts and packages.

5. Professional Conduct & Anti-Spam

Unsolicited messaging, abusive behavior, harassment towards students, recruiters, or academic supervisors will result in direct account messaging restrictions or full suspension.
